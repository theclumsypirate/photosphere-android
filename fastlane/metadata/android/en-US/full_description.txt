Point your phone around you and end up with a photo you can step inside.
Photo Sphere guides you frame by frame, fires the shutter itself when you
hold still, and stitches everything into one 360 photo with the metadata
viewers need. Export it to your gallery, share it, or save every frame as
its own photo.

No tripod, no second app, no cloud: capture, stitching and export all run
on the device, and the app has no network permission at all. You can also
import photos from your gallery and stitch those, re-stitch a sphere in
another style (Balanced, Crisp, Careful), or walk around a person or object
for a 12-angle turntable set.
